
Occam's Razor is one of the central principles of modern scientific theory. The principle is based on the following definition:

>  The most straightforward theory is preferable to all others of several sufficient possible explanations for the same state of facts. In other words: **The simplest explanation is always the most probable**

Eliminating the probable possibilities approach leads in the wrong direction and results in considerable effort to solve the problem.

`The simplest explanation for the best approach to penetration testing is that we work with the information we can get`

### Rule of Thumb:
When Pentesting ,  along side the specific steps , always focus on the gneral concept being conveyed so you can adapt in similar situation, 

`e.g  once we are familiar with SQL injection and how it occurs, we may find the individual steps difficult at first. However, once we understand the overall concept, it will be easy for us to discover when a web application is vulnerable to SQL injections.`
